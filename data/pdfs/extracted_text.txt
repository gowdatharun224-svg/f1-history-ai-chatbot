question.

2021 Season Guide
A title fight decided at the final race
Drivers’ Champion: Max Verstappen (Red Bull) — 395.5 points, 10 wins.
Constructors’ Champion: Mercedes — 613.5 points.
Drivers’ runner-up: Lewis Hamilton finished second in the Drivers’ Championship with 387.5 points.
Season overview
The 2021 season featured a close, intense championship battle between Max Verstappen and Lewis Hamilton. Verstappen
secured his first Drivers’ Championship at the Abu Dhabi Grand Prix, the final round. Mercedes won the Constructors’
Championship, showing that the drivers’ and teams’ titles can go to different teams.
Quick facts
 Season length: 22 Grands Prix.
 Drivers’ champion: Max Verstappen; runner-up: Lewis Hamilton.
 Constructors’ champion: Mercedes.
 The title was decided at the final round in Abu Dhabi.
 The 2021 points total includes half-points in the final season total; use 395.5 for Verstappen and 387.5 for Hamilton.

2022 Season Guide
Red Bull takes control under new regulations
Drivers’ Champion: Max Verstappen (Red Bull) — 454 points, 15 wins.
Constructors’ Champion: Red Bull — 759 points.
Drivers’ runner-up: Charles Leclerc finished second in the Drivers’ Championship with 308 points.
Season overview
Formula 1 introduced a major technical-regulation change in 2022, including ground-effect aerodynamic cars intended to
improve close racing. Ferrari began strongly, but Red Bull and Verstappen became dominant as the season progressed.
Verstappen won a then-record 15 races in a season and Red Bull secured both championships.
Quick facts
 Season length: 22 Grands Prix.
 Drivers’ champion: Max Verstappen; runner-up: Charles Leclerc.
 Constructors’ champion: Red Bull.
 Ferrari’s early challenge faded as Red Bull delivered stronger results over the season.
 The Japanese Grand Prix was the event at which Verstappen clinched the title.

2023 Season Guide
A record-setting Verstappen and Red Bull season
Drivers’ Champion: Max Verstappen (Red Bull) — 575 points, 19 wins.
Constructors’ Champion: Red Bull — 860 points.
Drivers’ runner-up: Sergio Pérez finished second in the Drivers’ Championship with 285 points.
Season overview
Red Bull dominated the 2023 season. Verstappen won 19 of the 22 Grands Prix and accumulated 575 points, one of the
most dominant campaigns in the sport’s history. Red Bull won 21 of the 22 races and secured the Constructors’
Championship. Pérez’s second place gave Red Bull a one-two finish in the Drivers’ standings.
Quick facts
 Season length: 22 Grands Prix.
 Drivers’ champion: Max Verstappen; runner-up: Sergio Pérez.
 Constructors’ champion: Red Bull.
 Verstappen scored 575 points and won 19 races.
 Sergio Pérez scored 285 points and finished second in the Drivers’ Championship.
 Red Bull won 21 of the 22 Grands Prix.

2024 Season Guide
Verstappen wins the drivers’ title; McLaren wins the teams’ title
Drivers’ Champion: Max Verstappen (Red Bull) — 437 points, 9 wins.
Constructors’ Champion: McLaren — 666 points.
Drivers’ runner-up: Lando Norris finished second in the Drivers’ Championship with 374 points.
Season overview
The 2024 season was more competitive than 2023. Verstappen built an early lead and secured his fourth consecutive
Drivers’ Championship, while McLaren’s strong results from Lando Norris and Oscar Piastri helped the team win its first
Constructors’ title since 1998. Ferrari also mounted a strong challenge in the Constructors’ standings.
Quick facts
 Season length: 24 Grands Prix.
 Drivers’ champion: Max Verstappen; runner-up: Lando Norris.
 Constructors’ champion: McLaren.
 Verstappen clinched the Drivers’ title at the Las Vegas Grand Prix.
 McLaren finished ahead of Ferrari and Red Bull in the Constructors’ Championship.
 Charles Leclerc won the Monaco Grand Prix.

2025 Season Guide
Lando Norris wins a close championship
Drivers’ Champion: Lando Norris (McLaren) — 423 points, 7 wins.
Constructors’ Champion: McLaren — 833 points.
Drivers’ runner-up: Max Verstappen finished second in the Drivers’ Championship with 421 points; Oscar Piastri finished
third with 410 points.
Season overview
McLaren entered 2025 as a leading team and retained the Constructors’ Championship. The Drivers’ title fight involved
Norris, teammate Oscar Piastri, and Verstappen. Norris ultimately won the Drivers’ Championship by two points over
Verstappen, with the title decided at the final round in Abu Dhabi.
Quick facts
 Season length: 24 Grands Prix.
 Drivers’ champion: Lando Norris; runner-up: Max Verstappen; third: Oscar Piastri.
 Constructors’ champion: McLaren.
 Norris scored 423 points; Verstappen scored 421; Piastri scored 410.
 Norris and Piastri each won seven Grands Prix.
 McLaren secured the Constructors’ Championship before the season ended.
